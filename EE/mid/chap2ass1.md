# SOLUTION

## PART 1: CALCULATE TOTAL COST (TC), SALES (TR), PROFIT, MR, AND MC

### Step 1: Complete the Cost and Revenue Analysis Table

**Formulas:**
- **Total Cost (TC)** = TFC + TVC
- **Total Revenue (TR)** = Price × Quantity = $50 × Q
- **Profit** = TR - TC
- **Marginal Cost (MC)** = Change in TC / Change in Q = ΔTC/ΔQ
- **Marginal Revenue (MR)** = Change in TR / Change in Q = ΔTR/ΔQ

### Marginal Cost (MC) and Marginal Revenue (MR)

**Key Observations:**
- **MC (Marginal Cost) = $30** (constant at all output levels)
  - This indicates a **perfectly elastic variable cost function**
  - Each additional unit costs exactly $30 to produce
  - Variable cost per unit = $300/10 = $30 per unit (constant)

- **MR (Marginal Revenue) = $50** (constant at all output levels)
  - This indicates a **perfectly competitive market**
  - Price = MR = $50 (firm is a price taker)
  - Each additional unit generates exactly $50 in revenue

---

### Profit Maximization Rule

Since **MR ($50) > MC ($30)**:
- The firm should continue producing and selling more units
- Profit increases as output increases
- The firm maximizes profit at the highest possible output level (Q = 50)

## PART 3: BREAK-EVEN POINT ANALYSIS

### Break-Even Point Calculation

**At Break-Even Point:**
- Total Revenue (TR) = Total Cost (TC)
- Profit = 0
- **BEP Quantity = 25 units**
- **BEP Sales = $1,250**

### Alternative BEP Calculation

**Formula Method:**
$$\text{BEP (Quantity)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Price - Variable Cost per unit}}$$

- Fixed Cost = $500
- Price = $50
- Variable Cost per unit = $300/10 = $30
- Contribution per unit = $50 - $30 = $20

$$\text{BEP} = \frac{500}{20} = 25 \text{ units}$$

$$\text{BEP Sales} = 25 \times 50 = \$1,250$$

✓ **Verified!**

1. **Break-Even Point:** The firm must produce and sell **25 units** to break even at a price of $50 per unit.

2. **Profitability:** 
   - Below 25 units: Losses occur
   - At 25 units: Break-even
   - Above 25 units: Profits increase

3. **Marginal Analysis:**
   - MR ($50) > MC ($30) consistently
   - This means the firm should produce at the highest possible quantity
   - Each additional unit contributes $20 to profit

4. **Market Structure:** 
   - Firm operates in a **perfectly competitive market**
   - Price equals Marginal Revenue ($50)
   - Firm is a **price taker**

5. **Profit Maximization:** 
   - Maximum profit occurs at **Q = 50 units**
   - Profit = $500 (in the given data range)
