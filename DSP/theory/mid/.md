# Problem 4.20 – Sampling and Reconstruction

**Given:**

The continuous-time signal \( x_c(t) \) has a **triangular Fourier transform**:

\[
X_c(j\Omega) =
\begin{cases}
\text{nonzero}, & |\Omega| \le \Omega_0 \\
0, & |\Omega| > \Omega_0
\end{cases}
\]

where  
\[
\Omega_0 = 2\pi(1000) \text{ rad/s} \quad \text{(bandlimited to 1000 Hz)}
\]

The discrete-time system is an **ideal lowpass filter**:

\[
H(e^{j\omega}) =
\begin{cases}
1, & |\omega| < \omega_c \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]

---

## (a) Minimum Sampling Rate for No Aliasing

### Step 1: Aliasing condition
Sampling causes spectral replicas spaced by sampling angular frequency:

\[
\Omega_s = 2\pi f_s
\]

To avoid overlap (no aliasing):

\[
\Omega_s > 2\Omega_0
\]

### Step 2: Nyquist choice
The minimum sampling angular frequency is:

\[
\Omega_s = 2\Omega_0
\]

Thus, the **minimum sampling rate** is:

\[
f_{s,\min} = \frac{\Omega_s}{2\pi} = \frac{2\Omega_0}{2\pi} = \frac{\Omega_0}{\pi}
\]

Substitute \( \Omega_0 = 2\pi(1000) \):

\[
f_{s,\min} = \frac{2\pi(1000)}{\pi} = 2000~\text{Hz}
\]

✅ **Answer (a):**  
\[
\boxed{f_s = 2000~\text{Hz}}
\]

---

## (b) Minimum Sampling Rate for \( y_c(t) = x_c(t) \) with \( \omega_c = \pi/2 \)

### Step 1: Frequency mapping between analog and digital domains

Relationship:

\[
\omega = \Omega T_s = \Omega \frac{2\pi}{\Omega_s}
\]

or equivalently:

\[
\Omega = \frac{\omega}{T_s} = \omega f_s
\]

where \( T_s = 1/f_s \).

---

### Step 2: Mapping the passband correctly

We must ensure the analog band \( |\Omega| \le \Omega_0 \) maps **within** the digital passband:

\[
|\omega| = |\Omega T_s| \le \omega_c
\]

At \( |\Omega| = \Omega_0 \):

\[
\Omega_0 T_s \le \omega_c
\]

\[
T_s \le \frac{\omega_c}{\Omega_0}
\]

\[
f_s \ge \frac{\Omega_0}{\omega_c}
\]

---

### Step 3: Substitute values

\[
\Omega_0 = 2\pi(1000), \quad \omega_c = \frac{\pi}{2}
\]

\[
f_{s,\min} = \frac{2\pi(1000)}{\pi/2} = 4000~\text{Hz}
\]

✅ **Answer (b):**  
\[
\boxed{f_s = 4000~\text{Hz}}
\]

---

### Notes:
- Part (a) ensures **no aliasing** (Nyquist rate = 2× highest frequency).
- Part (b) ensures **exact reconstruction** through a discrete-time lowpass filter of bandwidth \( \omega_c = \pi/2 \), hence a higher sampling rate.

---

### Summary Table

| Part | Condition | Formula | Result |
|------|------------|----------|---------|
| (a)  | No aliasing | \( f_s > 2f_{\max} \) | \( f_s = 2000~\text{Hz} \) |
| (b)  | Exact reconstruction (\( \omega_c = \pi/2 \)) | \( f_s = \dfrac{\Omega_0}{\omega_c} \) | \( f_s = 4000~\text{Hz} \) |
